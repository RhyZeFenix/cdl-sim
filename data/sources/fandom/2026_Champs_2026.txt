The '''Call of Duty League Championship 2026''' was the playoffs portion of the global professional [[New To Call of Duty/Franchising|franchised]] Call of Duty league operated by [[Activision]] that decided the World Champions of the ''[[Black Ops 7]]'' season. The event was hosted by [[FaZe Vegas]].

==Overview==
===Format===
*Double Elimination
*Best of 5 Series
*Best of 9 Series for Grand Finals
