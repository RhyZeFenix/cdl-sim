'''Call of Duty League 2026 - Major 2''' is the second [[Call of Duty League]] major of the [[2026 Season|2026 season]]. Hosted during [[DreamHack|DreamHack Birmingham 2026]], it will take place from March 27-29 at the National Exhibition Centre in Birmingham, England.

==Overview==
===Format===
*10 teams attend
*Seeding determined by [[Call of Duty League/2026 Season/Major 2/Qualifiers|Qualifier]] matches
*Play-In Bracket
*Double Elimination
*Best of 5 Series
*Best of 7 Series in Grand Finals
