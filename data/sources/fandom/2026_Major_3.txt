'''Call of Duty League 2026 - Major 3''' is the third [[Call of Duty League]] major of the [[2026 Season|2026 season]]. Hosted during [[DreamHack|DreamHack Atlanta 2026]], it will take place from May 15-17 at the Georgia World Congress Center in Atlanta, Georgia, United States. The event will be a [[Glossary_of_Call_of_Duty_Esports_Terms#Pro-Am|pro-am]] tournament featuring four [[Call of Duty Challengers/2026 Season|Challengers]] teams.

==Overview==
===Format===
*'''Group Stage'''
**16 teams
***12 Call of Duty League teams
***4 Challengers teams
**4 GSL groups of 4 teams each
**Best of 5 Series
**Top 2 teams in each group qualify for Playoffs
**Eliminated Challengers teams will compete in the [[Call of Duty Challengers/2026 Season/Atlanta Open|Challengers Open]]
*'''Playoffs'''
**Double Elimination
**Best of 5 Series
**Best of 7 Series in Grand Finals
