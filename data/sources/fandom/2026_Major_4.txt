'''Call of Duty League 2026 - Major 4''' is the fourth [[Call of Duty League]] major of the [[2026 Season|2026 season]]. Hosted by [[Paris Gentle Mates]], it will take place from June 25-28 <!---at the Moody Coliseum--->in Paris, France.

==Overview==
===Format===
*All teams attend
*Seeding determined by [[Call of Duty League/2026 Season/Major 4/Qualifiers|Qualifier]] matches
*Double Elimination
*Best of 5 Series
*Best of 7 Series in Grand Finals
