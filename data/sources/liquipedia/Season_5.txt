==About==
===Format===
*'''Regular Season''' - ''December 8th, 2023 - June 23rd, 2024''
** '''4-Stage Season'''
*** All Matches {{Abbr/Bo5}}
*** Each Stage comprised of Online Qualifiers and a Major
** '''Online Qualifiers'''
*** 7 matches played per team
*** Online Qualifier standings will be used to determine seeding in each Major for all 12 Teams
** '''Major'''
*** Double Elimination Bracket
** '''CDL Points'''
*** Teams will earn CDL Points for each match win
*** Addditional CDL Points distributed based on the performance in each Major
