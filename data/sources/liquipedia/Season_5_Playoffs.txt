==About==
<p style="max-width:990px; padding-top:15px">The '''2024 Call of Duty League Championship''' is the final event and the World Championship of the [[Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III|Modern Warfare III]] Competitive Season organized by [[Activision]]. Best teams throughout the CDL Season 5 have qualified to this event.</p>
===Format===
* <span class="bg-up">Top 8</span> teams from the [[Call of Duty League/Season 5#Regular Season|Regular Season]]
* Double Elimination Bracket
* All the matches are {{Abbr/Bo5}}, except for the Grand Finals which is {{Abbr/Bo9}}
