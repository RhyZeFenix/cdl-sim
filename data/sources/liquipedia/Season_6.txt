==Format==
*'''Regular Season''' - ''December 6th, 2024 - May 25th, 2025''
** '''4-Stage Season'''
*** All Matches {{Abbr/Bo5}}
*** Each Stage comprised of Online Qualifiers, a Minor and a Major
** '''Online Qualifiers'''
*** 7 matches played per team (Stages 1 & 2)
*** 5 matches played per team (Stages 3 & 4)
*** Online Qualifier standings will be used to determine seeding in each Minor and Major for all 12 Teams
** '''Minor'''
*** Single Elimination Bracket
** '''Major'''
*** Double Elimination Bracket
** '''CDL Points'''
*** Teams will earn CDL Points for each match win
*** Addditional CDL Points distributed based on the performance in each Minor and Major
