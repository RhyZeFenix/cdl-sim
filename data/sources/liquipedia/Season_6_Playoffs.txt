==About==
<p style="max-width:990px; padding-top:15px">The '''2025 Call of Duty League Championship''' is the final event and the World Championship of the [[Call of Duty: Black Ops 6|Black Ops 6]] Competitive Season organized by [[Activision]]. Best teams throughout the CDL Season 6 have qualified to this event.</p>

* All the matches are {{Abbr/Bo5}}, except for the Grand Finals which is {{Abbr/Bo9}}
