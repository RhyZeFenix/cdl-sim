==About==
<p style="max-width:990px; padding-top:15px">The '''2026 Call of Duty League Championship''' is the final event and the World Championship of the [[Call of Duty: Black Ops 7|Black Ops 7]] Competitive Season organized by [[Activision]]. Best teams throughout the CDL Season 7 have qualified to this event.</p>
