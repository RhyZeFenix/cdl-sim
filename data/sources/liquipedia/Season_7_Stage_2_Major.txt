==About==
===Format===
* Top 6 teams in [[Call of Duty League/Season 7/Stage 2|Qualifiers]] qualify for Playoffs
* Seeds 7-10 in [[Call of Duty League/Season 7/Stage 2|Qualifiers]] qualify for Play-Ins

* '''Play-Ins'''
** Single Elimination
** Winner qualifies for Playofffs
** All matches are {{Abbr/Bo5}}

* '''Playoffs'''
** Double Elimination
** All matches are {{Abbr/Bo5}}, except for Grand Finals which are {{Abbr/Bo7}}
